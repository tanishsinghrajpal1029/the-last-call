A quantum horror game

The Last Call

Your sister calls and asks you to let her in. The intercom shows her at both doors at once: one call, two entrances. Listen to both doors together and the interference pattern tells you who is calling. Trace the call and you learn where she is, but tracing destroys the interference.

What the circuit does

  
q0 holds the call. Its two states are the two doors. Ry(α) splits the call between the doors, P(θ) is the caller's hidden phase (Mira: 0, the mimic: a known offset), P(−φ) is your dial, and H recombines the doors at the handset. Measuring q0 gives a clear line (0) or static (1), with P(clear) = ½(1 + sin α cos(θ − φ)).

  
Trace adds a CNOT that copies “which door” onto q1 before recombining. You learn the door; the line becomes a coin flip at every dial, for Mira and the mimic alike.

  
Edit config.py to change shots. The doors, figures and voices are fiction: the circuit never processes audio. A classical computer can simulate this small circuit; the game is about the rule it teaches.
